ESPN Login

Private leagues need two cookies from ESPN: espn_s2 and SWID. Grab them once from a desktop/laptop browser, then paste below (they work on your phone after that). Public leagues can skip this — just toggle “Public league”.

📖 How to get your cookies in Brave — step by step
  1. In Brave, go to espn.com and log in to your fantasy account (do this first — the cookies only exist once you're signed in).
  2. Open the same tab's Developer Tools: press ⌥ Option + ⌘ Cmd + I. (Or menu ☰ → More Tools → Developer Tools.)
  3. In the panel that opens, click the Application tab along the top. If you don't see it, click the » chevron to reveal hidden tabs.
  4. In the left sidebar, expand Storage → Cookies, then click https://www.espn.com.
  5. In the list, click the Name column header to sort, then find the two rows named SWID and espn_s2.
  6. Click SWID, then double-click its Value and copy it (⌘ Cmd + C). Keep the { } braces. Paste it in the SWID field below.
  7. Do the same for espn_s2 — copy the whole long value and paste it exactly. Don't retype or “clean it up.”
  8. Tap Connect. Done.
